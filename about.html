About CraWings

CraWings Pizza Shop started as a small idea between friends who loved two things: great pizza and great wings. The founders believed that food should bring people together, whether it’s family, friends, or neighbors from the community. With that idea in mind, CraWings opened its doors with a mission to serve fresh, flavorful food made with simple, high-quality ingredients.

The name CraWings comes from the combination of two favorites on the menu: crispy chicken wings and handcrafted pizza. From the very beginning, the shop focused on making dough fresh every day, using rich tomato sauce, real mozzarella cheese, and toppings that customers love. Each pizza is baked until the crust is perfectly golden and the cheese melts into a delicious layer of flavor.

One of the most popular dishes at CraWings is the Classic Pepperoni Pizza, known for its crispy edges and perfectly balanced seasoning. Customers also love the Buffalo Wings, which are tossed in the shop’s signature spicy sauce and served hot and fresh. For those who enjoy something simple and traditional, the Margherita Pizza—with fresh basil, tomato sauce, and mozzarella—has become another favorite.

But CraWings Pizza Shop is more than just food. The restaurant’s goal is to create a welcoming place where everyone feels at home. Whether someone stops by for a quick slice, orders wings for game night, or brings their family for dinner, CraWings wants every meal to feel special.

Today, CraWings continues to serve its community with the same passion it started with—great pizza, great wings, and great moments shared around the table.
